## DAR: Dynamics, Automation, Results

We build AI assistants for small and medium businesses. Not chatbots for the website: assistants that take routine off the owner's desk.

**What we automate**

| Process | What the assistant does |
|---|---|
| Client calls | brief before the call, transcript, recap to the client, follow-up, CRM update |
| Sales follow-ups | reminds, writes the next message, hands warm leads to a human |
| Reporting | daily and weekly reports from your tables and chats, delivered to Telegram |
| Content | posts and replies in your voice, approved before publishing |
| Hiring | screens candidates, books interviews, sends test tasks |

**Open source**

- [consulting-skills](https://github.com/darautomation/consulting-skills): Claude skills for consultants, coaches, psychologists, realtors, lawyers and financial advisors. Pre-call brief, transcription, client recap, follow-up, progress tracking.

**How we work**

1. AI check-up of your business, 7 minutes: [finsave.online/ai/check](https://finsave.online/ai/check/?src=github-profile)
2. Diagnostic session: where the owner is the bottleneck, which 5 processes go first, savings in hours and money
3. Pilot on one process in 2-4 weeks, fixed price agreed upfront
4. Support and new processes monthly

Our own practice runs on these assistants every working day: calls, reports, content, client service.
